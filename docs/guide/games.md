# Games on the iPhone

When the phone isn't connected to any of your computers, because their home is away or none is linked yet,
two small games are waiting in the corners of the screen. They show while the Imps are on the stage and no
round is running. Both are optional, and your best score is kept.

## Embers (bottom right)

A little flame shows in the bottom-right corner. Tap it for a **45-second round**.

- Embers rise through the dungeon in waves (a column, a slant, a chevron). Fling the Imps through them.
- Catching several in one flight builds a **combo** (reach ×5 for "Ablaze!").
- Two Imps that bonk mid-air score a bonus. A rare blue wisp is worth five.
- Take every ember of a wave for a **"Perfect wave!"** bonus that grows with each wave in a row.
- Twice a round a banner announces a special phase:
  - **Ring time**: rings of eight embers that pay a bonus when one flight takes the whole ring.
  - **Wisp storm**: slower blue wisps, five points each.
  - **Golden shower**: gold sparks worth three each, falling.
- The heat rises over the round, and the last 10 seconds are a **Blaze** where every point counts double.
- A green kindling spark adds 5 seconds when caught (a round never runs past 75 seconds).
- Dark smoke costs 2 points and your combo if an Imp flies through it.
- A round with **no smoke hit** earns **"Clean burn"** (+15).
- A round where **no ember got away** earns **"Nothing got away"** (+25, a golden finale). An ember that leaves
  the top uncaught counts as a miss. The bonus needs at least 20 embers judged in the round.
- No smoke rises during a special phase.
- The end card shows the best combo, perfect waves and what got away.
- The first round starts with a short lesson: fling an Imp through three embers, read a card with the rules,
  then a 3-2-1 countdown. Skip it any time. **How to play** on the end card brings it back.
- The edges always bounce during a round, so nobody gets thrown to another device by accident.

## Echo (bottom left)

A ring of four dots opens a memory game. Four runes (flame, leaf, drop, moon) sit on the dungeon floor.

- The Imps hop to them and light them in a sequence, each with its own tone and its own pattern of taps.
  You tap the same sequence back.
- Every round adds one rune.
- A slip makes the Imps dizzy and costs one of **three lives**. The same sequence plays again.
- A clean round pays double, and every fifth wins a life back.
- It works with one Imp or many. The tones are soft and follow the silent switch.
- The first round is a hands-on lesson (watch two runes light, then tap them yourself), then a card with the
  rules and a countdown. Skip it, or use **How to play** later.
- Imps can't be picked up or tossed away while it runs, and tapping one opens no chat.
