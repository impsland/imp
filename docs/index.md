# Imp

Imp is a small companion that lives on your desktop and your phone. Each Imp has its own
character, its own Brain (the model it thinks with), and its own tools.

New to Imp? Start here:

- [Getting started](/guide/getting-started) installs Imp and runs your first chat.
- [Features](/guide/features) lists everything an Imp can do.
- [Services](/guide/services) connects a model, local or in the cloud.
- [Privacy](/guide/privacy) says what stays on your Mac.

Using Imp across devices and sharing:

- [Imps on other devices](/connecting/phone) covers the iPhone, doors, visits and trust.
- [Sharing setups](/sharing/setups) explains kits: import, export and review.
- [The Den](/sharing/den) is the gallery of shared Imps.

Building and reference:

- [Building from source](/development/building) and [Testing](/apps/testing).
- [Architecture](/architecture/) describes the layers and the repository.
- [Core models](/concepts/models), [Imp Link](/connecting/link) and [Kit format](/sharing/kits) are the reference pages.
- [Design](/design/) explains the look and the rules behind it.
