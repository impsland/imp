# Imp

Imp is a small companion that lives on your desktop and your phone. Each Imp has its own
character, its own Brain (the model it thinks with), and its own tools.

Start with these pages:

- [Models](/concepts/models) explains the core types.
- [Bubble (chat)](/concepts/bubble) explains how you talk to an Imp.
- [Transfer by shaking](/concepts/transfer) explains how an Imp moves between your devices.
- [Imp Link 2](/connecting/link) explains how devices connect.
- [Kit format](/sharing/kits) explains how characters and animations are shared.
- [Design](/design/) explains the look and the rules behind it.
